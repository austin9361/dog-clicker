name: Dog Clicker Ultimate
author: Austin
desc: Click to pet your dog, buy delicious treats, and build puppy parks to attract all the dogs!

Settings
building cost multiplier: 1.15

Resources
*bark
name: Bark | Barks
desc: The sound of happy puppies. Used to buy items!

Buttons
*petDog
name: Pet Dog
desc: Give your good boy some love!
on click:get 1 bark

Buildings
*dogTreat
name: Dog Treat
desc: Automatically generates barks by keeping your dog excited.
cost: 15 bark
passive:get 0.5 bark

*puppyPark
name: Puppy Park
desc: A massive park that attracts dozens of puppies to bark automatically.
cost: 100 bark
passive:get 4 bark

Upgrades
*goldenBrush
name: Golden Brush
desc: Grooming your dog makes clicks twice as effective.
cost: 50 bark
passive:multiply click power of petDog by 2

*premiumKibble
name: Premium Kibble
desc: High-quality food makes Dog Treats 50% more effective.
cost: 250 bark
passive:multiply production of dogTreat by 1.5
