Let's make a game!
name: Dog Clicker Ultimate
author: Austin
desc: Click to pet your dog, build massive canine empires, and watch your furry friend grow!

Settings
building cost multiplier: 1.15
background: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1548199973-03cce0bbc87b?q=80&w=1920

Resources
*bark
name: Bark | Barks
desc: The sound of happy puppies. Used to buy items!

*imageTimer
name: Image Timer
always hidden

*dogState
name: Dog State
always hidden

Buttons
*petDog
name: Pet Dog
desc: Give your good boy some love!
on click:get 1 bark
icon: https://i.imgur.com/vH93O4w.png

Buildings
*dogTreat
name: Dog Treat
desc: Automatically generates barks by keeping your dog excited.
cost: 15 bark
on tick:get 1 bark

*puppyPark
name: Puppy Park
desc: A massive park that attracts dozens of puppies to bark automatically.
cost: 100 bark
on tick:get 4 bark

*doggyDaycare
name: Doggy Daycare
desc: A premium care facility where dogs play and bark all day long.
cost: 500 bark
on tick:get 15 bark

*boneFactory
name: Bone Factory
desc: High-tech industrial automated factory molding thousands of chew toys.
cost: 3000 bark
on tick:get 75 bark

*cloningLab
name: Cloning Lab
desc: Advanced bio-engineering structure designed to safely replicate the ultimate good boys.
cost: 25000 bark
on tick:get 450 bark

Upgrades
*goldenBrush
name: Golden Brush
desc: Grooming your dog makes clicks twice as effective.
cost: 50 bark
passive:multiply click power by 2

*premiumKibble
name: Premium Kibble
desc: High-quality food makes Dog Treats twice as effective.
cost: 250 bark
passive:multiply efficiency of dogTreat by 200%

*spriteRotator
name: Sprite Rotator Engine
desc: Hidden background process that handles image rotation logic.
always hidden
on tick:get 1 imageTimer
on tick:if imageTimer>=15 get 1 dogState
on tick:if imageTimer>=15 lose 15 imageTimer
on tick:if dogState>2 lose 3 dogState
on tick:if dogState=0 set icon of petDog to https://i.imgur.com/vH93O4w.png
on tick:if dogState=1 set icon of petDog to https://i.imgur.com/YwN9VmW.png
on tick:if dogState=2 set icon of petDog to https://i.imgur.com/kS9ZtZ4.png
