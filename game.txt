Let's make a game!
name: Galactic Frontier Idle
author: Space Pioneer
desc: Gather stardust, build automated colony pods, and engineer warp drives to colonize the galaxy!

Settings
building cost multiplier: 1.15

Resources
*stardust
name: Stardust | Stardust
desc: Floating cosmic particles used to forge structures and power ships.

Buttons
*gatherStardust
name: Gather Stardust
desc: Deploy your solar sails to catch stray stardust.
on click: earn 1 stardust

Buildings
*colonyPod
name: Colony Pod
desc: A self-sustaining biodome that houses automated mining drones.
cost: 15 stardust
passive: earn 0.5 stardust per second

*solarArray
name: Solar Array
desc: Massive space mirrors that capture intense stellar radiation.
cost: 100 stardust
passive: earn 4 stardust per second

Upgrades
*plasmaSails
name: Plasma Sails
desc: Upgrades your manual gathering array. Double your clicking power.
cost: 50 stardust
passive: multiply click power of gatherStardust by 2

*droneOptimization
name: Drone Optimization
desc: Upload an AI update to your Colony Pods to make them 50% more efficient.
cost: 250 stardust
passive: multiply production of colonyPod by 1.5
